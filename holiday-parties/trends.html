Questions

Holiday party trends, answered

What are the biggest corporate holiday party trends for 2026?

Fewer but better parties, an intentional arrival moment, hands-on craft stations, zero-proof and inclusive menus, recognition moments, photo-ready backdrops, and glam looks like disco, Neo Deco and opera-style draping. Micro parties, smaller team dinners across the season, are also on the rise.

When should we book our company holiday party?

As early as you can. Christmas falls on a Friday in 2026, so Thursdays and Fridays on December 3–4, 10–11 and 17–18 book first. For a full-service December party, aim to lock your date six to eight weeks out. If December is full, a January kickoff party is a strong alternative.

What is the 2026 color of the year for holiday décor?

Pantone's 2026 Color of the Year is Cloud Dancer (PANTONE 11-4201), a soft white. It pairs well with Pinterest's "Cool Blue" trend for a winter wonderland palette, or with evergreen and warm neutrals for a quieter look.

What are the big activation moments in 2027?

Lunar New Year on February 6, Super Bowl LXI at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles on February 14 (Valentine's Day), Ramadan and Eid al-Fitr in February and March, the FIFA Women's World Cup in Brazil from June 24 to July 25, and the run-up to the LA28 Olympics in July 2028.

Do you plan corporate holiday parties outside Orange County?

Yes. We plan and produce holiday parties across Orange County, Los Angeles and San Diego, in offices, hotels, private venues and outdoor spaces.
