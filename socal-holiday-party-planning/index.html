Company holiday parties and corporate events

From a 30-person office party in Irvine to a 500-guest gala in Downtown Los Angeles, we build company holiday parties around your budget and your people. Choose passed holiday bites, a buffet, a plated dinner or live chef stations; add a mixologist bar (you buy the alcohol from our shopping list and we bring the bartenders, mixers, garnish and ice); then layer in a holiday photo backdrop, a gingerbread decorating station, a cocoa cart, a DJ or a jazz trio. When to book a holiday party in Southern California

  1. September to October: the best time to book December Fridays and Saturdays for company holiday parties.
  2. Early November: prime December weekend dates are usually booked; weekday and January dates stay open longer.
  3. 21 days out: final headcount and menu are due.
  4. Party day: we set up, host the party and leave the space spotless.

Holiday party questions

Is there a minimum?

Drop-off catering starts at a $500 food and beverage subtotal. Buffets start at 50 guests or $5,000. Plated dinners and chef stations start at $3,000 in food. Seated tablescapes are built for 75 or more guests. For a team of 10 to 40, holiday cocktail hour, a mixologist bar, cocktail styling and an activity station work beautifully.

How far ahead should we book?

Prime December Fridays and Saturdays usually book by early November. We send proposals within one business day, so you can move quickly once you have a date.

Can you come to our office or venue?

Yes. We set up in offices, lobbies, restaurants, private homes, clubs, rooftops and outdoor sites across Orange County and Los Angeles, and coordinate load-in, parking and venue rules for you.

How does alcohol work?

You buy the alcohol, we handle everything else. We send a shopping list sized to your guest count, then bring certified mixologists, the bar, mixers, garnish, ice and your signature holiday cocktails. Bartenders check ID, and every bar includes zero-proof drinks.

Can you handle dietary needs?

Vegetarian is always included. Vegan, gluten-aware, dairy-free and allergy-conscious options are available with advance notice, and every bar includes zero-proof drinks.

Do you provide insurance and paperwork?

We carry $2M general liability and provide a certificate of insurance naming your company or venue, a W-9, and company invoicing against your purchase order.

How do deposits and payment work?

A 50% retainer reserves the date. Final headcount and menu are due 21 days before the event and the balance 30 days before.

What is the 22% charge?

It's a service and production charge on catering, bar service, décor, rentals and florals. It covers planning, sourcing, staffing, setup, breakdown and cleanup. It is not a gratuity.

What if plans change?

More than 90 days out, only the retainer is kept. From 31 to 90 days, 75% of the contract is due, and within 30 days, 100%. Custom florals and special orders can't be refunded once ordered.

Can kids come?

Absolutely. Our kids collection covers company family days with Santa, gingerbread, games and a kids corner, all hosted by our team.
