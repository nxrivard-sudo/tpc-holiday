Questions hosts ask

Straight answers.

Is there a minimum?

Drop-off boxes start at a $500 order. Plated dinners and chef stations start at $3,000 in food, and buffets at 50 guests or $5,000. For a smaller party of 10 to 30, a cocoa cart, holiday bites and a gingerbread station make a whole party.

Can you do it at my house?

Yes. Most of our holiday parties are in homes, backyards, clubhouses and beach houses across Orange County, Los Angeles and San Diego. We plan load-in, parking and power with you, and bring heaters and a weather plan for outdoor parties.

How far ahead should I book?

December weekends book first, usually by early November. We send proposals within one business day, so you can move quickly once you have a date.

How does alcohol work?

You buy the alcohol, and we handle everything else. We send a shopping list sized to your guest count, then bring the mixologist, the bar, mixers, garnish, ice and your signature holiday cocktails. Bartenders check ID, and every bar includes zero-proof drinks.

Can you plan a kids-only party?

Absolutely. Our kids parties are hosted start to finish for 8 kids or more, with kid-friendly food, gingerbread, games, Santa or a movie night, and take-home treats.

Can you handle dietary needs?

Vegetarian is always included. Vegan, gluten-aware, dairy-free and allergy-conscious options are available with advance notice.

How do deposits and payment work?

A 50% retainer reserves the date. Final headcount and menu are due 21 days before the party and the balance 30 days before.

What is the 22% charge?

It's a service and production charge on catering, bar service, décor, rentals and florals. It covers planning, sourcing, staffing, setup, breakdown and cleanup. It is not a gratuity.

What if plans change?

More than 90 days out, only the retainer is kept. From 31 to 90 days, 75% of the contract is due, and within 30 days, 100%. Custom florals and special orders can't be refunded once ordered.
